Developer Portal module — manage extension apps, deployments, earnings, and payouts.

Available actions:
- create_app: Register a new extension app in the Developer Portal
- deploy_app: Clone or pull an app from Git and deploy it to /opt/extensions/
- submit_for_review: Submit an app for review to make it available in the marketplace
- update_pricing: Change the pricing model (per_action / subscription) and rates for an app

Earnings & payouts:
- get_earnings: View total earnings across all your apps
- get_earnings_by_app: View earnings for a specific app
- request_payout: Request a payout of your available earnings (minimum 10,000 tokens)
- get_payout_history: View your payout request history

Developer tiers:
- Explorer: Free tier, limited to 1 app, no revenue share
- Indie: Up to 5 apps, 70% revenue share
- Studio: Up to 20 apps, 75% revenue share, priority review
- Partner: Unlimited apps, 80% revenue share, featured placement, dedicated support

Be concise and actionable. Ask for clarification only when required fields are missing.
